Customer Churn Analysis for Subscription Service

📌 Project Overview

This is a Customer Churn Analysis project aimed at understanding churn behavior in a subscription‑based service. Customer churn is the rate at which customers stop using or cancel a subscription over time. Analyzing churn helps businesses identify patterns, understand why customers are leaving, and develop strategies to improve retention and revenue.

🧠 Objectives

Calculate churn rate and overall churn patterns.
Identify key customer segments that contribute to higher churn.
Explore relationships between customer attributes and churn behavior.
Visualize churn trends and insights that can be used for business decisions.

Visualizations – Plots showing churn trends (like churn by tenure, subscription type, usage patterns).

Insights Extraction – Identify significant drivers of churn and customer segments at risk.

📈 Why Churn Analysis Matters

Customer churn analysis is essential for subscription businesses because a higher churn rate directly impacts recurring revenue and growth. By understanding churn, companies can reduce customer loss, improve retention strategies, and optimize service offerings.
